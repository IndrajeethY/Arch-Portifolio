STARTUP SOUND SETUP
===================

To add the Linux startup sound to your portfolio:

1. Find or download a Linux startup sound (e.g., Ubuntu startup sound, Arch boot sound, etc.)
   - Recommended: Short sound (1-3 seconds)
   - Format: MP3, WAV, or OGG

2. Rename the file to: startup-sound.mp3

3. Place it in this public/ directory

4. The sound will automatically play when you log in to the desktop

Note: Due to browser autoplay policies, the sound may not play on the first visit
until the user interacts with the page. This is normal browser behavior.

Suggested sources for Linux startup sounds:
- Ubuntu startup sound (classic)
- Arch Linux boot beep
- Generic Linux startup chime
- Custom sound of your choice

Volume is set to 30% to avoid being too loud.
